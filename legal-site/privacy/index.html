Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

JackParty Piraten is an offline event-planning tool. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens when you contact support.

Information stored by the app

Reveal cards, cover lines, lock settings, rehearsal records, and archive events are stored locally on your Apple device. The developer does not receive this content. The app does not include advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or other privacy-sensitive device data. Host-confirmed arrival is a manual action performed inside the app.

Deleting app data

You can remove locally stored content through Settings → Delete All Data. Removing the app from your device also removes its local data, subject to any device backups you control through Apple services.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the address, message, and any details you choose to include. This information is used only to answer your request, investigate a reported problem, and maintain the related correspondence. Do not include private reveal content in a support message.

This website

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser details, and security signals to deliver and protect the site under its own privacy terms. JackParty Piraten does not run analytics or advertising on this site.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
